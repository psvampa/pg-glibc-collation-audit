--- the tie detector (query 6b), both nodes -------------------------------
The sort keys ARE the UTF-8 encodings of the code points, which is what
codepoint_collation means: strxfrm is the identity. So the "true" above is
real byte order and not tied weights rescued by PostgreSQL's strcmp
tie-break -- the third reading query 2 warns about.

  One correction to an earlier claim about this template: PostgreSQL breaks
  strcoll ties itself by comparing bytes, so ORDER BY on a libc collation is
  already a total, plan-independent order. Verified two ways -- empirically
  across seqscan and index-scan plans on both versions, and in the PostgreSQL
  source: the strcmp tie-break sits in both the row comparator and the
  sortsupport comparator in every release from 13 to 18, abbreviated keys
  return "indeterminate" rather than "equal" on a tie and so cannot bypass
  it, and nondeterministic collations (the only case that skips the
  tie-break) are rejected for every provider except ICU. The COLLATE "C" is
  a no-op. Tie order IS unspecified in sort(1), which resolves tied lines by
  input order, so feed both sides byte-identical input.
